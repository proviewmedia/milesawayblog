<p><strong>A complete, step-by-step guide based on my experience.</strong></p>
<p>I recently went through the full process of obtaining my <em>Certificado de Ciudadanía Puertorriqueña</em>, and I can tell you firsthand: the result is incredible. Having the official certificate feels meaningful, personal, and powerful, especially if Puerto Rico is part of your identity.</p>
<p>[banner]</p>

<p>Because I was born in Puerto Rico, my case was straightforward. I submitted my original birth certificate along with the required forms and digital stamps, and everything came back smoothly in my return envelope. But the steps can still be confusing if you have never dealt with Puerto Rico's government systems before.</p>

<p>This guide will walk you through exactly what I did, what you must do, and how to avoid mistakes. If anything is unclear while you are applying, the Department of State can help. Their contact information is listed at the bottom of this guide.</p>

<h2>1. Download the Colecturía Digital App</h2>
<p>All digital stamps must be purchased through the mobile app, not a computer. Download <strong>Colecturía Digital</strong> from the <strong>App Store</strong> or <strong>Google Play</strong>. Search for: <strong>Colecturía Digital</strong>.</p>

<h2>2. Create Your Account</h2>
<p>Open the app and tap <em>¿No tienes cuenta? Regístrate</em>. Enter your name, last name, and email. Create a username and password using:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Letters</li>
  <li>Numbers</li>
</ul>
<p>No symbols, punctuation, or underscores. A verification code will be emailed to you — enter the code to complete registration. Check your junk folder if it does not appear.</p>

<h2>3. Buy the Required Digital Stamps</h2>
<p>Once you are logged in:</p>
<ol>
  <li>Tap <strong>Sellos</strong> from the bottom navigation</li>
  <li>Select <strong>Departamento de Estado</strong></li>
  <li>Find <strong>Certificado Ciudadanía Puertorriqueña</strong></li>
  <li>Tap <strong>Comprar</strong></li>
</ol>
<p>The app automatically adds the two required stamps:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>5120</strong> — three dollars and twenty-five cents ($3.25)</li>
  <li><strong>5131</strong> — twenty-six dollars and seventy-five cents ($26.75)</li>
</ul>
<p>That is all you need to apply.</p>

<h2>4. Complete Your Order</h2>
<p>Tap <strong>Completar Orden</strong> and enter your credit card information.</p>
<p><strong>Important:</strong> Your billing address must match your credit card statement exactly. If anything differs, the system rejects the payment.</p>
<p>After payment, your stamps appear immediately in the app, and you also receive them by email as bar codes. <strong>Print the email bar codes</strong> — they must be included in your package.</p>

<h2>5. Gather Your Required Original Documents</h2>
<p>Your application must contain <strong>original documents</strong>, not photocopies or scans. Your required documents depend on how you qualify.</p>

<h3>If you were born in Puerto Rico (my situation)</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Original Puerto Rico birth certificate</li>
  <li>Valid government ID</li>
  <li>Completed application form</li>
</ul>

<h3>If applying through a parent or grandparent</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Your original birth certificate</li>
  <li>Their original Puerto Rico birth certificate</li>
  <li>Documents proving the family link</li>
  <li>Valid government ID</li>
  <li>Marriage certificates if last names changed</li>
  <li>Court documents for legal name changes</li>
  <li>Any supporting lineage documents</li>
</ul>

<h3>For complex lineage cases</h3>
<p>Include any official evidence required by law.</p>
<p>The Department of State will return all originals.</p>

<h2>6. Prepare Your Application Package</h2>
<p>You will need two large shipping envelopes. Large envelopes keep documents flat and protect them from damage.</p>

<h3>Large Envelope A: Your Outgoing Package</h3>
<p>Place all of the following inside the package — all documents need to be printed:</p>
<ol>
  <li>Completed application form (don't forget to print — download the <em>Certificado de Ciudadanía Puertorriqueña</em> form)</li>
  <li>All required original documents, such as:
    <ul>
      <li>Birth certificates</li>
      <li>Valid IDs</li>
      <li>Parent or grandparent birth certificates</li>
      <li>Marriage certificates</li>
      <li>Legal name change documents</li>
      <li>Official lineage documents</li>
      <li>Any other required proofs based on your case</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li>Printed digital stamp bar codes (Sello 5120 and Sello 5131)</li>
  <li>A large pre-addressed return envelope</li>
</ol>

<h3>Large Envelope B: Your Return Envelope</h3>
<p>This is what the Department of State will use to return your original documents and your official Puerto Rican Citizenship Certificate. Prepare it as follows:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Write your full mailing address clearly</li>
  <li>Add accurate postage or a prepaid Priority label</li>
  <li>Leave the envelope empty</li>
  <li>Place it inside Envelope A</li>
</ul>
<p>Seal Envelope A securely.</p>

<h2>7. Mail Your Application</h2>
<p>Send your complete package to:</p>
<p><strong>Department of State</strong><br>PO Box 9023271<br>San Juan, PR 00902-3271</p>
<p>They will mail everything back to you using the pre-addressed return envelope you included.</p>

<p>[banner]</p>

<h2>8. Need Help or Have Questions?</h2>
<p>If anything is unclear, or if your situation is different from mine, you can contact the Department of State directly:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Phone:</strong> (787) 291-9292</li>
  <li><strong>Email:</strong> <a href="mailto:servicioalcliente@colecturiadigital.com">servicioalcliente@colecturiadigital.com</a></li>
</ul>
<p>They can verify requirements, help with stamp issues, or answer specific questions about your case.</p>

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2026

Overview

Miles Away Blog ("we," "us," or "the site") is a travel blog operated from Las Vegas, Nevada. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit milesawayblog.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep this simple and honest — the same way we approach everything on this site.

Information We Collect

We collect very little, and we never sell your data. The information involved falls into two categories:

  • Analytics data (automatic): Like most websites, we use Google Analytics to understand which stories people enjoy. This includes anonymized data such as your general location (country/region), device type, browser, and the pages you view. It does not personally identify you.
  • Information you give us (voluntary): If you email us or use the contact form, we receive your name, email address, and message so we can reply. If you subscribe to the newsletter, we store your name and email to send updates.

Cookies

We use cookies only for analytics (via Google Analytics) to measure site traffic and improve content. You can disable cookies at any time in your browser settings without losing access to the site. We do not use cookies to track you across other websites.

Affiliate Links & Partnerships

Miles Away Blog participates in affiliate programs and brand partnerships. This means some links on the site — particularly in gear reviews and credit card articles — are affiliate or partner links. If you click one and make a purchase or sign up, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

Here's our promise: we only recommend products, cards, and services we have genuinely used and believe in. A commission never changes our honest opinion. Sponsored or partnered content is always presented transparently.

Third-Party Services

We rely on a small number of trusted third parties to run the site:

  • Google Analytics — traffic measurement (Google Privacy Policy)
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting
  • Affiliate partners — when you click an affiliate link, that partner's own privacy policy applies to your activity on their site

Your Choices

  • Disable cookies in your browser to opt out of analytics.
  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in any email.
  • Email us to request that we delete any personal information you've shared.

Children's Privacy

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. When we do, we'll revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information? Email us at cuadventuresllc@gmail.com or use our contact page.
